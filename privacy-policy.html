1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how ExamReady ("we", "our", or "the application") protects your privacy when you use our mobile application and web utility. We are dedicated to delivering privacy-first, free tools for candidates and learners preparing application materials for examinations and recruitment portals.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

Because ExamReady is an offline-capable client-side utility:

  • No Personal Information: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, postal addresses, or government identification numbers.
  • No Document Uploads: Photos, signatures, thumb impressions, and certificates remain strictly in your device memory during editing and are cleared when you close the app.
  • No Biometric Data: The app does not analyze facial geometries or fingerprint biometric templates; it performs graphical scaling and compression only.
  • No Financial Data: The app is 100% free with no in-app purchases, payment gateways, or subscriptions.
  • No Third-Party Analytics or Trackers: We do not include tracking SDKs, advertising networks, or user profiling services.

3. Device Permissions & Usage

ExamReady requests only the minimal permissions required to function:

  • Camera (android.permission.CAMERA): Optional. Used solely if you choose to take a photo or scan a certificate directly using your device camera. Photos are processed in memory and never shared.
  • Photos & Media Storage (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / Storage): Used solely to allow you to pick the photo, signature, or document you want to crop and compress from your device gallery.
  • Internet (android.permission.INTERNET): Used solely to load the initial web interface and to allow you to visit official resources (such as official exam portal guidelines and our educational YouTube channel). All document processing functions completely offline.

4. Data Storage & Security

All image cropping, aspect ratio adjustments, size optimizations (KB reduction / enhancement), Date-of-Photo (DOP) stamping, and PDF creation take place locally within your web browser runtime or mobile WebView memory using HTML5 Canvas and JavaScript. When you download a prepared file, it is saved directly to your device's local Downloads folder.

5. Children's Privacy

ExamReady is designed for general audiences, students, and competitive examination candidates. We do not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

6. Google Play Data Safety Compliance

In accordance with the Google Play Developer Program Policies:

  • Data Collection: None (No user data collected).
  • Data Sharing: None (No user data shared with third parties).
  • Security Practices: All processing is strictly local (on-device).
